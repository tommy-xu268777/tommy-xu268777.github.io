I am a master's student in Computer Science at Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI). I received my B.Eng. in Communication Engineering from Nanjing University in 2024.

My research focuses on **AI Security** and **Reasoning**. I am interested in securing LLM agents and multi-agent systems, and in understanding and improving the reliability of reasoning in large language models. My goal is to build AI systems that reason reliably and act securely.

## Research Interests

- **AI Security:** LLM agent safety, malicious behavior detection, and robustness in multi-agent systems.
- **Reasoning:** Reliable reasoning in large language models, multi-step problem solving, and reasoning in tool-using agents.
